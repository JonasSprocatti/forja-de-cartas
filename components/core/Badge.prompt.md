**Badge** — a compact status pill in Cinzel.

```jsx
<Badge variant="gold">VIP</Badge>
<Badge variant="pub">Pública</Badge>
<Badge variant="priv">Privada</Badge>
<Badge variant="neutral">Planeswalker</Badge>
```

`gold` is the filled VIP/emphasis treatment; `pub` (green outline) and `priv` (muted outline) mark a card's visibility; `neutral` is a plain hairline tag for type/layout labels.
