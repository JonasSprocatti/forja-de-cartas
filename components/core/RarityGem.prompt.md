**RarityGem** — the expansion symbol; its colour is the rarity language of the whole app.

```jsx
<RarityGem rarity="mítica" />
<RarityGem rarity="rare" />
```

● Comum (ink) · ◆ Incomum (silver) · ★ Rara (gold) · ✦ Mítica (ember-orange). Accepts Portuguese or English rarity names. Sits in the card's type bar, right-aligned, and in gallery meta rows.
